04Method

From orbit to arrival.

  1. I

    Orbit

    Explore before you commit. Every course opens with free sample lessons, so you can circle a few constellations and see what pulls you in.

  2. II

    Launch

    Start with a small cohort on a set date. Foundations come first, with a weekly live seminar and short exercises you can fit around work.

  3. III

    Navigate

    Every course ends in a project reviewed by a mentor. You get written feedback, a second attempt if you want it, and a clear sense of what to learn next.

  4. IV

    Arrive

    Pathways finish with a capstone that pulls everything together — a piece of work you can show to an employer, a client or yourself.
